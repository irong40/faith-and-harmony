# SOP: Cemetery Documentation Shoot

Marker inventory and site mapping for historical cemeteries, aligned to the
**Virginia Historical African American Cemetery and Graves Fund** documentation
requirements. Added 2026-07-25.

The client's money depends on a **defensible count of eligible graves**. That
count is the deliverable. Everything else supports it.

## Eligibility, so the flight captures the right things

- **Eligible cemetery:** established before **January 1, 1948** for the interment of African Americans
- **Eligible grave:** a person born **before 1900** (any death date), or born 1900 or later and **interred before 1948**
- Reimbursement runs **per eligible grave per year**, currently **$5**
- Applications are filed by the property owner, a qualifying charitable organization, or the local government. SAI is the vendor to the applicant, not the applicant

> ⚠️ Rates and deadlines are set per funding cycle. Confirm against the current
> DHR grant manual before quoting or before a figure goes in an application.

## Pre-Shoot Checklist

- [ ] Confirm permission from the property owner or the cemetery association in writing
- [ ] Confirm whether burials are marked, unmarked, or both. Unmarked burials change the scope
- [ ] Weather. Dry, and after a mow if one can be arranged. Tall grass hides flush and fallen markers
- [ ] **Season matters.** Late fall through early spring, with leaves down, is materially better under tree cover
- [ ] Sun angle. Low, raking light reveals carved inscriptions that flat mid-day light washes out
- [ ] Airspace check, LAANC if required
- [ ] Ask about family visits and services that day. Schedule around them
- [ ] SOP Gatekeeper completed in Trestle

## Equipment

- Matrice 4E with RTK, for the mapped site
- Medium tele, for legible inscription capture without descending onto the markers
- Ground camera, for stones that will not read from the air
- Chalk is **not** used. Never mark a historic stone

## Conduct on Site

This is a burial ground and often an active one for the descendant community.

- Ground crew walks the paths and the mown lanes, not over graves, where those are discernible
- No equipment cases, tripods, or landing pads set down on a grave
- If family members are present, introduce yourself and explain the work before flying
- Launch and land from a road, drive, or clear area outside the burial ground

## Flight Planning

### Site map pass
- Full-site orthomosaic at fine GSD, RTK on
- 75 to 80 percent overlap
- Extend the boundary beyond the fence or tree line, so encroachment is visible

### Marker pass
- Lower altitude or medium tele for individual marker capture
- Every marker photographed well enough to read where the stone still reads
- Under canopy, expect to supplement from the ground

## Shot List

- [ ] Full-site orthomosaic
- [ ] Individual capture of every visible marker
- [ ] Oblique of each marker face where the inscription is carved (raking light)
- [ ] Condition capture: displacement, sinking, fragmentation, biological growth
- [ ] Boundary: fences, walls, tree lines, encroachment from adjacent parcels
- [ ] Access: gates, drives, parking, and what a maintenance crew could get in
- [ ] Context shot of the site in its surroundings
- [ ] Ground-level capture of any marker the air could not resolve

## Deliverables

- [ ] Georeferenced orthomosaic of the cemetery
- [ ] **Marker inventory:** sequential ID, coordinates, photograph, and transcription where legible
- [ ] Condition photograph set per marker
- [ ] Boundary and encroachment documentation
- [ ] Deliverables index and full flight log
- [ ] Before and after imagery, on maintenance-cycle repeat visits

Marker condition uses the **severity set** (L / M / H) from the report system.

## What SAI Does Not Determine

- **Eligibility of a specific grave.** SAI documents birth and death dates where the stone records them. Whether a grave qualifies is the applicant's determination against the fund's rules
- **Unmarked burials.** Aerial imagery does not find them. Where unmarked burials are suspected, that needs ground-penetrating radar and a subsurface specialist. Say so rather than implying the count is complete
- **The count is of documented markers**, and the report states it that way

## Pricing Reference

Not yet set. Scale with marker count and canopy cover, since both drive time.
A site under heavy tree cover can take twice the capture effort of an open one.

## Safety Notes

- Uneven, sunken ground is the main trip hazard. Watch footing, not the screen
- Old cemeteries have unstable stones. Do not lean on or steady a marker
- Tree cover means GNSS degradation at low altitude. Keep line of sight and stay conservative
